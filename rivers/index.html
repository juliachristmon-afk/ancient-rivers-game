THE EXPLORER’S NOTES

A little context matters.

This learning map uses a longitude/latitude projection. Coastlines and river courses are hand-generalized for classroom use, not navigation. Country names mark approximate locations; they are not political boundaries.

Ancient civilizations did not share today’s country borders. Questions use specific river sections or sites when a river crosses several countries. Dates are approximate.
